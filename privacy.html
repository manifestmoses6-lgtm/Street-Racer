Street Racer – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Street Racer (the "App", package name com.streetracer.app) handles information. The App is provided by [YOUR NAME OR STUDIO NAME] ("we", "us").

1. The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect certain device information as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves game data directly on your device, such as your coins, unlocked cars and levels, upgrades, stars, best times, high score, settings (music, sound effects, theme), and the date of your last daily reward. This data is stored locally and is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show interstitial ads and rewarded video ads. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and software information (model, operating system version, language);
  • information about how you interact with ads and the App (for example, whether an ad was shown or watched).

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device). If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

4. Internet access

The App requests internet access only so that ads can be loaded and shown. The game itself works without sending your game data anywhere.

5. Children's privacy

Street Racer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can help.

6. Data sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. We do not receive or store personal information about you, so we have nothing to share, sell, or retain on our servers. Data processed by Google AdMob is handled by Google as described in section 3.

7. Security

Game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Ads are delivered over secure connections provided by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. You can erase the data stored on your device at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have additional rights regarding data processed by Google; please see Google's privacy policy linked above.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Please review it occasionally.
